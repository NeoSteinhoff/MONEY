The business is a premium DTC scented candle brand, designed and shipped from Dubai, built around a quiet-luxury visual identity and a Middle Eastern scent vocabulary — oud-cedar, amber-musk, rose-saffron — that international brands cannot authentically claim. Dubai is the most fragrance-obsessed city on earth. The AED 149–219 middle tier — with credible brand design and local scent identity — is under-served by local operators and undefended by the imported names. ### QC Inspection Checklist (8 product-specific points)

1. **Fragrance accuracy:** burn one unit from each box in the batch; scent must match approved sample within subjective ±10% intensity across top, middle, and base notes
2. **Burn pool:** must reach full-width melt pool by hour 3 with no tunnelling or raised wax ridges around edges
3. **Glass integrity:** zero chips, cracks, or bubbles on vessel; exterior surface uniform with no mould seam marks visible from 50cm
4. **Fill level:** wax fills to within 1.5cm of jar rim — measure and confirm consistency across all 300 units; variations >5mm indicate inconsistent pour process
5. **Label alignment:** label centred horizontally and level (±2mm tolerance); no adhesive bubbles, lifting corners, or ink smear
6. **Wick centring:** wick must sit within 2mm of centre at base; photograph before accepting; off-centre wick = uneven burn = customer complaint
7. **Box condition:** kraft outer undamaged with clean fold creases; tissue insert present and unfolded; SAMT sticker seal intact on tissue
8. **Soot test:** burn for 30 minutes, hold white paper 10cm above flame — zero black marks is the pass threshold; any soot indicates incorrect wick gauge or fragrance oil contamination

## SECTION 5 · BRAND IDENTITY

### 3 Name Options

**Option 1: SAMT (سَمت)**
- Etymology: Arabic for "silence" and "stillness" — also the astronomical root of "zenith" (السَمت), the highest point of the sky directly above. Carries connotations of calm, elevation, and depth.
- Why it fits: Candles are purchased for atmosphere — the specific feeling of a quiet room, a lit space, stillness after a long day. SAMT owns that emotional moment without naming it explicitly. Globally pronounceable, two syllables, no hard sounds.
- Domain: samt.com [ASSUMED taken — domain registrar search required; samt.co / samt.store available with high probability] [NOT CHECKED]
- Instagram handle: @samthome / @samtcandles [NOT CHECKED]

**Option 2: NOOR (نور)**
- Etymology: Arabic for "light" — one of the 99 names of God in Islam, a female given name, and the most universally understood Arabic word across all MENA markets.
- Why it fits: Candle = light. The semantic connection is direct. NOOR is also the name of Dubai's annual light festival (Noor Dubai), giving an instant cultural anchor.
- Domain: noor.com [ASSUMED taken]; noorhome.com / noorcandles.com moderate availability likelihood [NOT CHECKED]
- Risk: "Noor" names at least 40 consumer brands across UAE/GCC — high genericness penalty.

**Option 3: NADHR (نَضْر)**
- Etymology: Arabic for "radiant," "luminous," or "fresh and flourishing" — used in classical Arabic poetry to describe something glowing from within.
- Why it fits: More unusual than NOOR, less generic, gives the brand room to own a unique phonetic identity.
- Pronunciation risk: NAH-dhr is unfamiliar to non-Arabic speakers — use "NADHR — radiant" in all English-facing copy to solve this.

### Bilingual Ruling

**Yes — Arabic is legally required on all consumer product packaging in the UAE.** Under UAE Consumer Protection Law (Federal Law No. 15 of 2020) and UAE Cabinet Resolution No. 66 of 2023 on Product Safety, all consumer goods sold in the UAE must display the product name, ingredients, instructions for use, and safety warnings in Arabic. Candles fall under consumer goods regulated by ESMA (Emirates Authority for Standardisation and Metrology). The product label and insert card must include in Arabic: the fragrance name, wax composition, burn instructions, and safety warnings ("keep away from flammable materials, children, and pets"). This is a compliance requirement, not optional. Non-compliant products can be removed from sale. [BENCHMARKED — UAE Consumer Protection Law / ESMA enforcement standards]
