The business is a quiet luxury padel accessories brand founded and shipped from Dubai, selling a structured two-racket tour bag DTC at AED 420, positioned in the vacant space between mass-market technical padel gear (Bullpadel, Adidas Padel at AED 265–480, logo-heavy and performance-branded) and streetwear-graphic padel lifestyle labels (PSF Collective, Padeloupe — loud prints, oversized fits, no editorial restraint). ### QC Inspection Checklist (8 Points — Run Before Accepting Any Production Batch)
1. **Shell material check:** Inspect 10 random units for canvas weave consistency, colour match to approved swatch, and absence of visible weave flaws
2. **Thermal compartment seal:** Test zip closure on the racket compartment for a full, gap-free seal on 10 random units — any gap = reject that unit
3. **Heat retention spot test:** Place a thermometer probe in the racket compartment of 3 random units, leave in direct sun for 2 hours, compare to ambient — compartment interior should track at least 8–10°C below external ambient
4. **Stitching integrity:** Check all load-bearing seams (strap attachment points, base corners) on 15 units for even, doubled stitching with no loose threads
5. **Leather trim attachment:** Confirm trim is riveted or box-stitched (not glued only) to the canvas shell on all sampled units
6. **Zipper cycle test:** Open/close each of the 3 main zippers 20 times on 10 random units, checking for smooth glide and no snagging
7. **Load-bearing strap test:** Load a sample bag with 2kg (approximating 2 rackets + shoes), suspend by the strap for 10 minutes, check for stitching pull or strap stretch beyond spec
8. **Colour and patch alignment:** Under bright light, confirm the debossed brand patch is centred within 2mm tolerance and colourway matches the approved Pantone reference across the full batch

### What Kills This in 30 Days — 3 Failure Scenarios
