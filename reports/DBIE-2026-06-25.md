## SECTION 1 · THE THESIS

The business is a UAE-born quiet-luxury home fragrance brand anchored in oud — the region's defining scent — sold direct-to-consumer via Shopify and grown through Instagram, gifting channels, and a 60-person promoter network. Neo's promoter network — CRM-backed and proven in event contexts — converts directly into a corporate gifting pipeline at Ramadan and UAE National Day, the two highest-volume gifting moments in the calendar. The design sensibility required to win this category (clean, restrained, gallery-quality packaging) is precisely the skill set Neo already demonstrates. This is a brand-executes-better story, not a product-innovation story, and that is the highest-probability path to sustainable margin at a 17-year-old's capital ceiling.

### QC Inspection Checklist — 8 Points

1. **Scent throw test:** Burn one unit in a 20 sqm enclosed room for 2 hours; oud scent must be detectable at 5m+ from the candle without approaching it
2. **Burn time verification:** Full burn test on one unit — count hours to wax depletion (target: 50+ hours for 200g)
3. **Wax pool evenness:** By second burn session, wax pool reaches within 5mm of glass edge all around; tunnelling = reject batch
4. **Soot production:** White tissue at 30cm above flame after 1 hour — less than 10% grey discolouration acceptable
5. **Label adhesion:** 5 minutes in a humid environment (UAE summer-standard humidity) — label must not peel, bubble, or shift
6. **Vessel integrity:** Check base for chips, rim for sharp edges; hold to light for air bubbles in glass
7. **Wick placement:** Wick centred ±2mm; pre-trimmed to 6mm height
8. **Fragrance batch consistency:** Smell 5 units from same batch — scent must be identical unit-to-unit; any variation indicates batch contamination; reject

### Bilingual Packaging Ruling

**YES — Arabic labelling is legally required on all consumer products sold in the UAE.**

Under UAE Federal Law No. 24 of 2006 on Consumer Protection and ESMA Cabinet Decision No. 3/2012 on General Requirements for Goods, all consumer products must include Arabic-language labelling covering: product name, ingredients/composition, country of origin, manufacturer/importer name and contact, safety warnings, and net quantity. This applies to candles (classified as consumer household goods). Failure to comply risks customs clearance rejection and retail sale prohibition. Action: Engage a UAE-licensed Arabic translation and label design service (not Google Translate) — cost AED 300–500 one-time — before first unit ships.

1. **Hook (word-for-word):** *"You looked at ATHAR. Here's why 200+ Dubai apartments smell like this now."*

4. **Psychological trigger:** Social proof via quantity + urgency + reminder of pre-existing interest
5. **CTA:** *"Order now. AED 195. Free delivery in 24 hours."*
6. **Funnel stage:** Warm retargeting (3-day website visitors)

**AD 5 — Hot Retargeting (Cart abandonment)**
1. **Hook (word-for-word):** *"Your ATHAR candle is still waiting. Burn time: 60 hours. We'll drop it to your door today."*

6. **Funnel stage:** Hot retargeting (add-to-cart, no purchase, 48-hour window)

**AD 6 — Wildcard (Gifting trigger, seasonal)**
