### QC Inspection Checklist — 8 Points (Run Before Accepting Any Batch)
1. **Glass integrity:** Hold each bottle against strong light — reject any unit with visible air bubbles, seam cracks, or asymmetric base
2. **Fill volume:** Weigh 10 random units versus target; reject batch if any unit varies more than 3% from 50ml fill weight
3. **Atomiser spray pattern:** Test 5 units at 15cm — should produce a fine mist; reject any clogging or sputtering
4. **Cap click seal:** Press and release cap on 20 units — should click firmly; invert and hold — reject any cap that falls under gravity
5. **Label alignment:** No tilt greater than 0.5mm; check 20 units under direct light for adhesive bubbling
6. **Scent match to approved sample:** Open-blind test — smell 5 random production units next to your signed sample vial; any unit with a perceptibly different opening note triggers a full batch smell-check
7. **Longevity field test:** Spray one production unit on skin, go outdoors in UAE heat; check projection at 4 hours and 6 hours — must project at arm's length at 4 hours minimum
8. **Box fit and seal:** Bottle must seat without rattle on inlay; magnetic seal sticker must contact cleanly; tissue paper must fully conceal bottle when lid closes

**Failure 2: Ad CTR below 0.8% after AED 600 spend on both cold creatives.**
Early warning signal: If neither Ad 1 nor Ad 2 achieves CTR >0.8% after 6,000 impressions each (approximately AED 180 spend per ad), the hook is not generating scroll-stop. **Failure 3: Product fails the heat-longevity claim.**
Early warning signal: If the first three Instagram Story posts from promoters or customers do not mention longevity, or if any comment specifically says the scent faded quickly, the core product promise is unvalidated. This brand's entire white-space claim is heat performance. If the formula doesn't deliver 6+ hours of projection in July UAE heat, the entire competitive position collapses into just another attractive-packaging fragrance at AED 245. Test the formula personally in the hottest conditions possible — 12pm, outdoors, direct sun — before committing to the 150-unit production run.
