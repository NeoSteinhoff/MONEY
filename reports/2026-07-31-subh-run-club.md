## SECTION 1 · THE THESIS

The business is SUBH ("dawn" in Arabic), a quiet-luxury run-club apparel and accessories brand headquartered and shipped from Dubai, launching with a signature technical performance cap at AED 135–165 and expanding into vests, singlets, and cushioned running socks — deliberately positioned in the pared-back, monochrome, Japanese-minimalist aesthetic lane that every current UAE competitor has left vacant. Nobody owns the quiet, monochrome, editorial aesthetic that the run-club member who also cares about quiet luxury actually wants to wear at 6am and again at brunch after.
