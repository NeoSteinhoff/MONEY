Fit rationale: short, phonetically simple in both English and Arabic mouths, ownable, carries genuine dual-market meaning rather than surface-level cultural styling. ### Bilingual/Arabic Packaging Legal Ruling
**Yes, Arabic is required.** Under Federal Decree-Law No. 5 of 2023 on Consumer Protection, suppliers must provide accurate, clear product information — composition, country of origin, care instructions — in Arabic, either Arabic-only or bilingual Arabic/English, with Arabic font size not smaller than the English text; production/expiry dates (where relevant) cannot be applied via sticker and must be on the original packaging [LIVE DATA — UAE Consumer Protection Law summary, Federal Decree-Law No. 5/2023, via legal-guide search 2026]. SITTA's ruling: bilingual packaging, Arabic set at equal or larger point size than English on all mandatory disclosure fields (composition, country of origin, importer details), while the brand-voice insert-card copy leads with English given the ICP's English-first bilingual profile — legal compliance is non-negotiable on the disclosure fields specifically, not on the entire card's creative copy.
