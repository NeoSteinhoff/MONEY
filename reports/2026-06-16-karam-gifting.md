### Bilingual Consideration

**Arabic on packaging: YES, legally required.** Under UAE Consumer Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 5 of 2023, Article 11) and UAE Cabinet Resolution No. 70 of 2020, products sold in UAE must display key consumer information in Arabic, including product name, country of origin, and any expiry information. The KARAM brand name must appear in both Latin script and Arabic (كرم) on the primary box. All expiry-date products (dates, honey) must have Arabic label with best-before date. The insert card must offer an Arabic version on request or include at minimum a bilingual greeting line. Recommended: produce dual-script packaging from Day 1 — it is a legal requirement and simultaneously an aesthetic asset that reinforces the brand's Arabic identity.

## SECTION 7 · UNIT ECONOMICS & FINANCIAL MODEL

### Unit Economics — SKU 1: The Occasion Box (AED 249)

**Landed cost per unit:** AED 55.40 (contents + box, before last-mile) [ESTIMATED — Section 4 build-up]
**Last-mile delivery:** AED 22.00 (Aramex Business account) [BENCHMARKED]
**Total cost of goods delivered:** AED 77.40

**Gross margin:**
(AED 249 − AED 77.40) ÷ AED 249 × 100 = **68.9%** [ASSUMED — based on Section 4 cost build]

**Estimated CAC:**
Formula: (CPM ÷ 1,000) ÷ CTR ÷ CVR = CAC
= (AED 22 ÷ 1,000) ÷ 0.018 ÷ 0.025
= AED 0.022 ÷ 0.018 ÷ 0.025
= AED 1.22 ÷ 0.025
= **AED 48.90 ≈ AED 49** [BENCHMARKED — CPM: AED 22 mid-range Instagram gifting; CTR: 1.8% above-benchmark for visual product; CVR: 2.5% mid-range UAE DTC]

**Contribution margin per unit:**
Gross profit per unit − CAC = (AED 249 − AED 77.40) − AED 49 = AED 171.60 − AED 49 = **AED 122.60** [ASSUMED]

**LTV (2-order assumption):**
AOV × 2 × gross margin % = AED 249 × 2 × 0.689 = **AED 343.12** [ASSUMED — 2 purchases per customer within 12 months; supported by UAE gifting calendar offering 6+ occasions per year to repeat buyers]

**LTV:CAC ratio:**
AED 343 ÷ AED 49 = **7.0:1** ✓ (well above the 3:1 minimum threshold)

**Break-even volume (monthly):**
Fixed monthly costs ÷ contribution margin per unit
Fixed costs: AED 2,650/month (Shopify AED 200/month; packaging design and brand assets amortised over 6 months: AED 300/month; SHAMS license AED 5,750/year = AED 480/month; miscellaneous: AED 500; phone + apps: AED 170; Klaviyo starter plan: AED 180; Loox reviews: AED 120)
Break-even: AED 2,650 ÷ AED 122.60 = **21.6 → 22 orders/month** [ASSUMED]

**Break-even ad spend:**
22 orders × AED 49 CAC = **AED 1,078/month** [ASSUMED]

**Minimum cash reserve:**
3 months fixed costs + 1 month ad spend = (3 × AED 2,650) + AED 3,000 = **AED 10,950** — within the AED 15,000 preferred capital ceiling.
