The business is a quiet luxury padel lifestyle apparel brand — technical polos, overshirts, caps, and a signature court bag — designed and shipped from Dubai, sold DTC at AED 180–320 per piece, positioned deliberately in the vacant space between mass-market performance padel wear (Adidas, Nike, generic UAE retailers at AED 120–250, functional but faceless) and the Euro technical-competition tier (Bullpadel, Varlion, Belén Bérbel at AED 300–600, built for tournament players, not for the court-to-brunch crowd that actually drives Dubai's padel culture). ### QC Inspection Checklist (8 Points — Run Before Accepting Any Finished Batch)
1. **Fabric integrity:** Check for snags, uneven weave, or visible seam puckering across 10 random units
2. **Embroidery placement:** No more than 3mm variance in chest-logo position across the batch; check thread tension for looseness
3. **Colourfastness:** Wash-test 3 random units 3 times; reject batch if visible fading or bleeding occurs
4. **Stitching strength:** Pull-test side seams and hem on 5 units; reject if any seam gives under moderate pull
5. **Sizing consistency:** Measure chest/length on 3 units per size against the approved spec sheet; reject batch if >1cm variance
6. **Breathability spot-check:** Wear-test one unit through a 90-minute match; note sweat-through pattern
7. **Label/tag accuracy:** Confirm size tags match the actual garment measurement, not just the stated size
8. **Packaging fit:** Folded garment must sit cleanly in poly bag with no visible embroidery distortion from folding
