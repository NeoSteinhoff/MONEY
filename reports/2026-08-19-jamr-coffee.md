### QC Inspection Checklist (8 Points — Run Before Accepting Any Production Batch)
1. **Roast date accuracy:** Confirm printed roast date on a random sample of 10 bags matches the actual production run date — reject any batch with pre-printed (non-batch-specific) dates
2. **Weight check:** Weigh 10 random units vs. 250g spec; reject batch if >3% variance from target
3. **Valve function:** Test 10 units — bag should degas visibly within 24 hours without collapsing or letting air back in when pressed
4. **Seal integrity:** Pull-test the pouch seal on 10 units; any seal that separates under moderate hand pressure = reject
5. **Cupping/taste consistency:** Brew 3 random bags from the batch against the approved sample profile; reject if 2+ show a materially different flavour, acidity, or body result
6. **Visual bean/grind check:** Inspect for roast-colour consistency (no scorched or underdeveloped beans) and absence of foreign matter; for pre-ground SKUs, check particle size against the approved grind sample
7. **Label/QR accuracy:** Scan the QR code on 5 random units — confirm it resolves to the correct batch page with correct roast date and origin; check bilingual (Arabic/English) label compliance
8. **Heat-exposure seal test:** Leave one sealed bag in direct UAE summer sun/car conditions for 2 hours, then inspect for seal degradation or valve failure before accepting the batch for hot-weather delivery
