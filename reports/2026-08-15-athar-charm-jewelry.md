### QC Inspection Checklist (8 Points)
1. **Clasp pull-test:** Every batch — test 10 random units to 2kg pull-force; any failure below that threshold rejects the batch (this is the #1 category failure mode identified in Section 2)
2. **Plating colour consistency:** Compare 10 random units side-by-side under direct sunlight — reject any batch with visible colour variance
3. **Water-submersion spot check:** Submerge 5 units for 24 hours, inspect for any discolouration or bubbling in the plating
4. **Jump ring integrity:** Confirm every jump ring is soldered closed, not simply pressed — visually inspect the seam under magnification on 15 units
5. **Chain link consistency:** Check for uneven links or weak solder points across the chain length on 10 units
6. **Packaging fit:** Charm/chain must sit securely in the branded pouch with no rattle or scratch risk against other components
7. **Material certificate match:** Cross-check the batch's accompanying material certificate against the original sample-approved spec (nickel/lead/cadmium-free)
8. **Engraving/detail sharpness:** Inspect laser-engraved detail on 10 random charms for crispness — reject any batch with blurred or shallow engraving
