### Bilingual Packaging Ruling

**YES — Arabic labeling is legally required** on all cosmetic and personal care product packaging sold in the UAE.

Under UAE Federal Law No. 15 of 2020 (Consumer Protection Law) and Cabinet Decision No. 10 of 2023 (Consumer Protection Executive Regulations), all cosmetic and personal care products must carry the following information in Arabic on packaging: product name, ingredients list (INCI nomenclature in Arabic), usage instructions, safety warnings, manufacturer/importer name and address, country of origin, net weight or volume, batch code, and expiry or best-before date. This is enforced by the UAE Ministry of Economy and ESMA (Emirates Authority for Standardization & Metrology). Non-compliant products can be seized at the border or removed from sale.

**Execution:** Work with UAE private-label manufacturer (Dehnee, Coral) who routinely produce bilingual labels compliant with ESMA requirements as standard practice. Budget AED 300–500 for bilingual label design and compliance check before first production run.
