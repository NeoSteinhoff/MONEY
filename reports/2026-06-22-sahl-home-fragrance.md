## QC Inspection Checklist (8 Points)

Before accepting any batch from any supplier:

1. **Scent cold throw:** Remove lid, hold at arm's length 30 seconds. Can you clearly identify the intended fragrance notes? If not, fragrance load is insufficient (<8% — request minimum 10%).
2. **Wax surface:** Flat and smooth? Sinkholes or bumps indicate cooling speed issues — affects burn quality.
3. **Wick centring:** Wick within 2mm of vessel centre. Off-centre wicks cause uneven burns.
4. **Glass vessel integrity:** Hold up to light — no microcracks, clouding, or bubbles in glass wall. Check rim for chips.
5. **First burn test (minimum 4 hours):** Full melt pool should reach vessel edge within 4 hours on first burn. Tunnelling = wick too small.
6. **Scent hot throw (at 1 metre in 15sqm room after 30 minutes burning):** Should be clearly perceptible. This is the most common consumer complaint — test every batch.
7. **Label adhesion:** Gently attempt to lift label edge with fingernail. Should not peel with normal handling. Test in mild humidity (bathroom, 10 minutes) — label must hold.
8. **Packaging drop test:** Drop sealed outer box from 1.2 metres onto hard floor. Candle must not shift, chip, or crack. If it does, add void fill insert.

## Bilingual Packaging

**YES — Arabic is legally required.**

Under UAE Consumer Protection Law (Federal Law No. 15 of 2020) and the implementing regulation (Cabinet Resolution No. 5 of 2017), product labels sold in the UAE must include the product name, ingredient/component list, country of manufacture, and UAE importer/distributor details in Arabic. For a scented candle sold D2C in the UAE, this means the insert card must be bilingual (Arabic right-to-left, English left-to-right) and the vessel label must display the product name and key details in both languages. Non-compliance carries administrative fines. Design note: bilingual text is an opportunity, not a constraint — Arabic script in Cormorant Garamond's weight class is visually striking.
