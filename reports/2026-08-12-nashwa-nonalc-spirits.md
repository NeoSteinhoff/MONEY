The business is NASHWA, a Dubai-formulated, Dubai-bottled line of 0.0% ABV botanical aperitifs and cocktail mixers sold DTC at AED 150–180 per 500ml bottle, distributed simultaneously through two channels: direct ecommerce and — this is the structural unlock — placement into Dubai's bars, hotel F&B outlets, and private event bar programs via Neo's 60-person promoter network, which is disproportionately staffed by people who already work nightlife, events, and hospitality. ### QC Inspection Checklist (8 Points)
1. **ABV verification:** lab certificate of analysis per batch confirming 0.0% ABV, not "trace alcohol" — non-negotiable for a product marketed as alcohol-free in a market with religious and health-driven non-drinkers
2. **Sugar content verification:** lab certificate confirming actual sugar/sweetener content stays under the 5g/100ml excise threshold
3. **Fill volume:** weigh/measure 10 random units against the 500ml spec; reject batch if >3% variance
4. **Cork seal integrity:** test 20 units for a firm, non-rattling seal; any loose cork = reject
5. **Colour consistency:** compare 5 random bottles against the approved formulation sample under bright light — botanical infusions oxidise visibly if inconsistent
6. **Taste match:** panel-taste 5 random bottles against the approved sample; any batch with 2+ units showing a noticeably different flavor profile = reject and request reformulation review
7. **Shelf-stability spot check:** hold one unit at UAE ambient summer temperature for 14 days, re-taste and re-inspect for sediment or cloudiness
8. **Label/box fit:** label alignment within 0.5mm tolerance; bottle must seat snugly in the outer box with no rattle
