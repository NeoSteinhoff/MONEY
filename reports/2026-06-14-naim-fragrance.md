### QC Inspection Checklist (8 Points — Run Before Accepting Any Production Batch)
1. **Bottle integrity:** Hold each bottle to light — check for air bubbles, seam cracks, or asymmetry in glass
2. **Fill volume:** Weigh 10 random units vs. spec weight; reject batch if >3% variance from 50ml target
3. **Atomiser spray pattern:** Test 5 units — should produce a fine, even mist at 15cm range; reject clogged or spluttering atomisers
4. **Cap seal:** Cap should click firmly with no rattle; test 20 units; any that fall off under gentle inversion = reject
5. **Label alignment:** No more than 0.5mm tilt acceptable; check under bright light for adhesive bubbles
6. **Scent match:** Smell 5 random bottles vs. your approved sample; any batch where 2+ units have a noticeably different opening note = reject and request reformulation check
7. **Longevity spot test:** Spray one unit on skin, check at 4 hours and 6 hours — must still project at arm's length at 4h
8. **Outer box fit:** Bottle must seat snugly in box with no rattle; tissue paper insert should conceal bottle fully when box closed
