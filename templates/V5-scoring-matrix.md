## Criterion 5 · CAC Efficiency (UAE Meta Ads)
**Weight: 80 points** · *Points = Score × 8*

What it measures: Contribution margin (retail − COGS − delivery − gateway fee) divided by steady-state CAC (Month 3+, not Month 1 with first-advertiser premium). Higher ratio = more sustainable paid acquisition.

**Anchors:**

| Score | CM:CAC Ratio (steady-state) | What this means |
|-------|----------------------------|-----------------|
| 1–2 | < 1.0 — losing money on every paid customer at steady state | |
| 3–4 | 1.0–1.5 — marginal. Very thin operating leverage | |
| 5–6 | 1.5–2.5 — average. Workable but needs strong LTV to be profitable | |
| 7–8 | 2.5–4.0 — good. Each customer generates meaningful contribution above acquisition cost | |
| 9–10 | > 4.0 — exceptional operating leverage. Product sells itself relative to cost of acquisition | |

*Month 1 CAC is typically 2–3× steady-state due to learning phase. Always model both.*

| Dropshipping | AED ~100 | AED 55 | 1.82:1 | **5** |
