## How to Score

Each criterion has anchor descriptions for scores of 2, 5, 8, and 10.
- Score 1–3: The criterion is a problem that materially increases risk or reduces viability.
- Score 4–6: Average. Neither a tailwind nor a headwind.
- Score 7–9: A genuine strength that improves the opportunity.
- Score 10: Exceptional. Best-case scenario, verifiably true.

Do not score 10 unless you can cite a live data source proving it.
