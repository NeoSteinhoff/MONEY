# Pre-Report Filter — 30-Minute Opportunity Kill Screen
**DBIE Engine V5 · Use this BEFORE starting a full report**

> A full V5 report takes 4–6 hours. This filter takes 30 minutes.
> If you score below 60/100 OR any kill question fires, close the idea and move on.
> Do not do a full report to "confirm" something you already know is wrong.

---

## PART A · Kill Criteria (6 questions — any YES = STOP)

> Answer each honestly. One YES ends this evaluation.

**K1. UAE import or sale restriction**
Is this product on UAE's prohibited or restricted import list? (Check Dubai Customs HS code lookup for your specific product.)
`[ ] YES → STOP` `[ ] NO → continue`

**K2. Regulatory registration you're not ready to file**
Does this product require DM registration, ESMA certification, or any UAE authority approval — AND are you unwilling or unable to wait 4–8 weeks for it before first sale?
`[ ] YES → STOP` `[ ] NO → continue`

**K3. Capital ceiling breach**
Quick estimate: initial inventory MOQ cost + air freight to UAE + Month 1 ad budget (minimum AED 12,000) + creative production (minimum AED 5,000) + fixed costs (AED 1,500). Does this total exceed AED 35,000?
`[ ] YES → STOP (exceeds safe launch budget)` `[ ] NO → continue`

**K4. Gross margin below 60%**
At your target UAE retail price (not inflated — what a customer would realistically pay), is the gross margin after COGS but before delivery below 60%?
`[ ] YES → STOP (margin too thin for UAE DTC unit economics)` `[ ] NO → continue`

**K5. Dominant UAE incumbent**
Does an established UAE-based brand (>10k Instagram followers, active in 2025–2026) already own this exact positioning and price point?
`[ ] YES → STOP unless you have a clear differentiation thesis` `[ ] NO → continue`

**K6. >40 hours/week solo execution in Month 1**
Estimate honestly: packaging, WhatsApp follow-up, content creation, supplier comms, customer service, bookkeeping. Does this brand require more than 40 hours/week in Month 1 for one person?
`[ ] YES → STOP (not executable solo at this stage)` `[ ] NO → continue`

## PART B · Sizing Questions (4 questions — score each 0–25)

> Do not guess. Spend 5 minutes per question verifying.

**S1. UAE demand signal (0–25 points)**

Check: Instagram hashtag volume for the product category in UAE, TikTok UAE hashtag count, and Google Trends UAE for past 12 months.

| Evidence | Points |
|----------|--------|
| Declining Google Trends + <50k Instagram posts + TikTok dead | 0–5 |
| Flat trends + 50k–500k Instagram posts | 6–12 |
| Growing trends + 500k–5M posts + active TikTok UAE | 13–20 |
| Explosive growth + viral TikTok format exists for this product + high UAE-specific search | 21–25 |

## Quick Reference — UAE Regulatory Kill Criteria by Category

| Product Category | Likely Trigger | Action |
|-----------------|----------------|--------|
| Cosmetics / skincare | DM registration required | Check [dm.gov.ae](https://dm.gov.ae) — plan 4–8 weeks |
| Food / supplements | MOIAT registration + halal cert | 8–16 weeks minimum; specialist required |
| Medical / health devices | MOHAP approval | Very long; avoid |
| Fragrance (personal) | None if manufactured in UAE | NAIM is clear |
| Jewellery | Import only; standard 5% duty + VAT | Clear |
| Apparel / accessories | Import only; standard 5% duty | Clear |
| Electronics | ESMA certification (TRA) | 4–12 weeks depending on product |
| Automotive accessories | Import only (most items); check for safety-critical parts | Mostly clear |
